# Management Commentary — Q2 2026 Close
**Document type:** Management commentary
**Owner:** Office of the CFO
**Last updated:** 2026-07-02

Revenue for the quarter closed materially below plan. The shortfall is concentrated in
the North region and in Product A.

Three explanations have been put to the executive team:

1. **Supply disruption.** The Halden Plant 2 incident constrained Product A availability
   from early May. Operations has confirmed the incident and its dates.
2. **Competitive price pressure.** Nexora's North campaign launched at the start of April
   and extended to East in mid-May. Sales leadership believes this is the earlier and
   broader driver.
3. **Softening demand.** Some regional leaders argue underlying demand weakened
   independently of competitor action, citing extended procurement cycles.

The executive team has explicitly asked the analytics function **not** to settle on the
supply disruption as the headline cause simply because it is the best-documented one.
The incident is well evidenced because operations logs it; competitive loss is poorly
evidenced because we do not systematically capture it. Evidence availability is not the
same as causal weight.

## Open questions the finance team could not answer

- What share of the North volume decline occurred before 4 May?
- Would the quarter still have missed plan if fulfilment had been normal?
- Are the customers lost in April the same accounts that later reported delivery issues?

## Data quality caveats

- Win/loss reason codes were optional until 1 July 2026 and are largely unpopulated.
- Competitor pricing is observed from public advertising, not contracted rates.
- Marketing spend was reallocated mid-quarter; channel attribution for May is unreliable.
