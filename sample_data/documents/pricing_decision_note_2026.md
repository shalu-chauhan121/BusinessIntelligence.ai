# Pricing Decision Note — North Region Defence
**Document type:** Management / commercial note
**Owner:** Pricing Committee
**Last updated:** 2026-05-06

Following Nexora's 2 April campaign, the pricing committee approved a tactical online
discount of up to 3% on Product A in the North region, effective the week of 13 April.

Outcome as at 6 May:
- Realised price per unit in North online fell as intended.
- Unit volumes did **not** recover; the discount did not arrest the decline.
- Margin per unit in North fell without a compensating volume response.

Recommendation recorded: a price-only response appears insufficient. The committee
requested a fuller analysis of whether the volume loss is price-driven, availability-driven,
or a structural loss of accounts, before extending the discount to other regions.
