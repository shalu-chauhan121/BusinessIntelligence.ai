# Market Intelligence Briefing — Q2 2026
**Document type:** Market / competitive intelligence
**Owner:** Strategy & Market Insights
**Last updated:** 2026-06-27

## Competitor activity

**Nexora** launched its "Switch & Save" campaign in the **North region on 2 April 2026**,
positioned directly against our Product A price point. Publicly advertised terms:

- 18% headline discount versus our list price on the comparable SKU
- 24-month price lock for customers migrating from a competitor contract
- Waived onboarding fee for Enterprise accounts above 50 seats

The campaign was **regionally scoped to North at launch**. Nexora extended the same
offer into the **East region in the week of 18 May 2026**. South and West have not
been targeted as of the date of this briefing.

## Channel observations

- Distributor feedback indicates Nexora funded additional retail-partner rebates in North
  from mid-April.
- Our own online discounting in North (introduced to defend share) reduced realised
  price per unit but did not restore volume.

## Analyst commentary

Third-party analysts estimate Nexora gained 4–6 share points in the North mid-market
during Q2. The estimate is directional; it is based on channel checks, not audited data.

## Known gaps in this briefing

- We do not have independent win/loss data for Q2 2026; the CRM win/loss fields were
  only made mandatory from 1 July 2026.
- We cannot separate customers lost to Nexora from customers who deferred purchase.
