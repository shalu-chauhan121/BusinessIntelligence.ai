# Operations — Supply Incident Report
**Document type:** Operations report
**Owner:** Global Supply Chain Operations
**Period covered:** April – June 2026
**Last updated:** 2026-06-30

## 1. Incident summary

On **4 May 2026** our tier-1 component supplier (Halden Components, Plant 2) declared
force majeure after a transformer fire halted two of three production lines. Plant 2
supplies the display sub-assembly used exclusively in **Product A**.

Allocation was cut to 38% of contracted volume from the week commencing 4 May.
Normal allocation resumed the week commencing **22 June 2026**.

## 2. Operational impact

| Impact area | Observation |
|---|---|
| Product A finished-goods inventory | Fell sharply from the week of 4 May; North DC hit hardest (regional buffer was already the thinnest in the network) |
| Stockout events | Rose steeply across all regions from the week of 4 May, peaking in late May |
| Order fulfilment rate | Deteriorated materially for Product A; other products unaffected |
| Backorders | Product A backorder queue peaked at 11 days in the North DC |

The North distribution centre was disproportionately affected because it was running
a 2.1-week cover position going into May, versus 4.4 weeks in South and West.

## 3. What this incident does NOT explain

The operations team wants to be explicit about scope, because the commercial team has
asked whether this incident explains the quarter.

- The incident **began on 4 May 2026**. Order intake and customer counts in the North
  region were already running below plan for the **whole of April**, before any
  allocation was cut and before any stockout was recorded.
- Products B and C were **not** supplied by Halden Plant 2 and had **no** allocation
  constraint at any point in the quarter. Their volumes in North still declined.
- Fulfilment for Product A in South and West degraded by a similar mechanism, but the
  revenue shortfall in those regions was much smaller than in North.

Our position: the incident is a **genuine and material contributor** to the Product A
shortfall, particularly from mid-May onwards. It is **not** a sufficient explanation for
the North region's full-quarter revenue decline.

## 4. Remediation

- Dual-sourcing qualification for the display sub-assembly started 12 June (18-week lead time).
- Proposal to raise the North DC minimum cover position from 2.0 to 4.0 weeks.
- No automated stockout early-warning threshold currently exists; stockouts were detected
  from the weekly ops review, roughly 9 days after they began to rise.
