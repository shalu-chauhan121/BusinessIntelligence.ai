# Regional Sales Review — Q1 2026
**Document type:** Business/sales report
**Owner:** Commercial Operations
**Last updated:** 2026-04-08

Q1 closed broadly in line with plan at group level. Two forward-looking risks were
recorded at the March review:

1. **North pipeline quality.** Late-stage pipeline coverage in North fell from 3.1x to
   2.4x during March. The regional director attributed this to "increased competitive
   tendering" and flagged that a competitor had begun quoting aggressively in the
   mid-market in late March, ahead of any formal campaign launch.
2. **Renewal concentration.** 38% of North Enterprise contract value came up for renewal
   in Q2 2026 — the heaviest renewal quarter in the region's history.

No supply or availability risk was recorded at the Q1 review. Product A inventory cover
was reported as "adequate but thin in North" (2.1 weeks versus a 3.5-week policy).

Action agreed in March: monitor North renewals weekly. This monitoring was not
implemented before the Q2 close.
